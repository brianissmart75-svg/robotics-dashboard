US Public Schools - Legal & Compliance

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to US Public Schools (uspubschools.org). The following documents outline our strict adherence to federal and state compliance requirements, ensuring a safe, equitable, and privacy-first environment for all students and educators.

Non-Discrimination Policy

US Public Schools is steadfast in its commitment to providing an inclusive educational environment. We do not discriminate on the basis of sex, race, color, national origin, age, disability, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, or any other legally protected status in any of our programs, administrative operations, or employment practices.

All students and staff have equal access to educational tools, AI resources, and administrative support. Any concerns regarding potential discrimination should be reported directly to the administration.

Data Privacy & Research Use Policy

The privacy of our students, educators, and staff is paramount. Data collected through the uspubschools.org portal—including demographics, performance metrics, behavioral data, and system interaction telemetry—is strictly used for internal educational administration and individual progress tracking.

Under no circumstances will student or user information be sold, shared with third parties, or utilized for external research, studies, or commercial AI model training. We adhere to robust data minimization principles, ensuring only necessary data is collected to provide educational services.

FERPA & COPPA Compliance

US Public Schools strictly complies with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • FERPA: Parents and eligible students have the right to review educational records, request amendments, and control the disclosure of personally identifiable information (PII).
  • COPPA: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent. All interactive tools deployed across our domains are designed with student safety and anonymity in mind.

Terms of Service & Acceptable Use

By accessing and utilizing the uspubschools.org domain, all users agree to engage respectfully and safely. Our digital infrastructure is actively monitored to ensure compliance with technology use guidelines. Malicious activities, cyberbullying, or attempts to compromise platform security will result in immediate access revocation and potential disciplinary action.
